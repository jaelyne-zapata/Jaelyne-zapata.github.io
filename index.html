About Me

I am currently pursuing my Master’s in Accounting at the University of Utah. My interest in accounting began in high school when I joined DECA and competed in the accounting category, placing first at both regional and state competitions and advancing to the national level. Through my education and professional experience, I have developed an interest in how technology can be used to solve business problems, improve processes, and support decision-making. I am particularly interested in artificial intelligence and data analytics and how these tools can be applied to accounting and business. As technology continues to play a larger role in the accounting profession, I want to develop my technical skills and better understand how AI can complement traditional accounting practices. My goal is to combine my accounting knowledge with technology and analytical skills to help organizations make informed decisions and improve their operations.
